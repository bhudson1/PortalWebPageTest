Portal sign-in

For a secured layer, register this GitHub Pages URL as an OAuth redirect in your Portal application item, then enter its Client ID. Staff sign in with their own Portal accounts.

This OAuth flow requires a valid Portal URL, a registered public-client application and a matching redirect URI. The service host and Portal host may be different.
